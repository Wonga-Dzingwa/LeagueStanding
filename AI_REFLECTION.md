# AI Reflection

Collaborating with the AI to build the league standings CLI was for the most part a smooth journey. What was the most important was the preparation, ensuring all requirements were understood and ambiguities resolved. This is where I had to make system decisions that it wasn’t able to. An example of this is where to draw the 10-week cutoff. I decided to go with when most teams had played 10 games, putting us in the 10th gameweek, even though not all the teams had played their 10th games yet due to postponements.

Once the requirements were defined, creating a step-by-step development plan was the next step. Once that was clear, it was a fairly frictionless process, just going through each step and ensuring the plan was being followed. Overall, the collaboration was successful.
